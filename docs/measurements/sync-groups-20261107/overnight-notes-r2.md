## Root cause of the coldboot stall (found live on smoke-r3, 18:33)

- All three detentd were followers in term 1 (`role 0`), with `peer_connections 0`, `peer_handshakes 0` and
  about 880 `peer_dropped` each. Peer mTLS never completed, so no leader was ever elected.
- `tests/detent_authority/tls_certificates.cmake` issues the CA and leaf certificates with `-days 1`. The fixture is
  generated by the `authority_tls_certificates` ctest fixture, and stage.sh only copied it in when it was missing.
  - The before tree's copy (from Detent/build) expired 2026-10-18 19:53 GMT.
  - The after tree's (S2 build-rel) expired 2026-11-06 06:56 GMT.
- The fixture had already expired before the first chain was queued (2026-11-04), so the coldboot path as staged
  could never have passed on the before arm.
- Fix (harness only): stage-noproof.sh `fresh_fixture` regenerates the fixture with the tree's own script before every
  arm (smoke and pairs) and asserts at least 2h of validity. Voter pins are computed from the fixture at run time,
  so the source state clones are unaffected.
- smoke-r3 was left running to record the harness's own error. Its 14 r3 pairs were pulled to .scratch/held-r3
  before they could cascade.
- r4 queued: smoke + 14 pairs, all via stage-noproof.sh.
